CUTWORK

Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

CUTWORK is a tool that turns long videos into short vertical clips. This policy explains what information CUTWORK handles and what it does with it. It covers the CUTWORK website, the web app at /app, the desktop clipper, and the CUTWORK posting tool that publishes clips to TikTok and Instagram.

Videos you clip

In the web app and the desktop clipper, your video is processed on your own computer. Transcription, finding moments and rendering clips all happen on your device. Your video is not uploaded to a CUTWORK server.

If you paste a video link into the web app, a link helper downloads that video so the app can open it. The downloaded file is kept only long enough to pass it to your browser and is deleted automatically within one hour. Only links from well-known video sites are accepted.

Information we do not collect

  • CUTWORK has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • CUTWORK does not use advertising trackers and does not sell or share personal information.
  • The web app may save a transcript in your own browser's storage so that reopening the same video is faster. It stays on your device and you can clear it by clearing your browser's site data.

Posting to TikTok and Instagram

The CUTWORK posting tool lets the owner of a TikTok or Instagram account publish their own clips to that account. When you connect an account, you sign in on TikTok's or Instagram's own page and approve the permissions they show you. CUTWORK never sees your password.

With your approval, the tool receives access tokens from TikTok or Instagram and uses them only to:

  • read your basic account information, such as your username, to confirm which account is connected;
  • upload the clips you choose, with the captions you write, to your own account.

These tokens are stored only on the computer that runs the posting tool. They are not sent to CUTWORK or to anyone else. The tool keeps a local record of what was posted (the clip name, platform, time and post link) on that same computer.

You can disconnect at any time by removing CUTWORK in your TikTok or Instagram app settings, which cancels its access, and by deleting the tool's token file.

Third-party services

When you use TikTok or Instagram through CUTWORK, their own privacy policies apply to the information they hold. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Children

CUTWORK is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
